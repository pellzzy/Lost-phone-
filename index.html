Lost your phone? Do these steps in order.

Your phone holds your bank, your WhatsApp, and your email. Work through this list from the top. Tap a step to tick it off.

Start with the first three steps. That is where thieves do the most damage, so don't wait.

Please avoid these

  • Paying anyone who calls or messages to say they found your phone.
  • Clicking links sent to you about "your phone's location". Many are scams.
  • Sharing OTP codes or PINs with anyone, including people who say they work for your bank.
